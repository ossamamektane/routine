Privacy Policy

Routine · Last updated November 16, 2026
In short: Routine doesn't collect, sell or share any information about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What Routine stores

The routines, bills, goals and notes you enter, when you check them off, and your settings. This is stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, it's also stored in your private iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it can sync to your other devices and come back if you reinstall. Only you can see it. The developer has no access to it and no copy of it. Apple's Privacy Policy covers iCloud.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone itself. Nothing is sent to a server.

What Routine doesn't do

  • No sign-up, login or user account.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code (SDKs).
  • No servers. The developer doesn't run any servers for Routine.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, health data or calendars.

Children

Routine collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete any item in the app, and the deletion syncs to your other devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that iPhone. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for your device in the Settings app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

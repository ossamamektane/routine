Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up or login. Routine saves everything on your iPhone and syncs it through your own iCloud.

How does sync work?

Sign in to iCloud on each device with the same Apple Account. Changes usually appear within a minute. If iCloud is off, Routine keeps working and saves everything on your iPhone. You can check the status in Routine → Settings.

I used the Bills app. Where are my bills?

Bills is now part of Routine. iOS doesn't let one app read another app's data, so add your bills again in the Money tab with +. Any routine, chore or subscription can have an amount, and Money shows what's paid, what's left this month and what's coming up.

My reminders don't arrive

Open Routine → Settings and make sure Reminders is on. If it says notifications are turned off, tap the button to open the Settings app and allow them. Also check that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing Routine.

What is "After I Wake Up"?

Morning routines can start from the moment you tap “I'm up” on Today, for example "Vitamins 30 minutes after waking". If you forget to tap it, Routine can ask once at a time you choose.

How do I delete my data?

Delete an item by swiping left on it in the Routines tab. The deletion syncs to your other devices. Deleting the app removes the data from that iPhone. The copy in your iCloud stays so it comes back if you reinstall.
